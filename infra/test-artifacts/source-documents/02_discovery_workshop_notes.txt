DAY 1 — CURRENT STATE WALKTHROUGH
================================================================================

OPENING CONTEXT (Maria Chen)
- Board approved $4.2M transformation budget for finance operations over 18 months.
- Primary driver: reduce close time from T+8 to T+5 while improving SOX readiness.
- P2P selected as wave 1 because it touches 60% of monthly transaction volume.

PROCUREMENT (James Okonkwo)
- "We have three different buying experiences. Coupa works for MRO catalog in the US, but EU still emails PDFs to buyers."
- Average 47 active buyers across regions; only 12 are certified on SAP ME21N.
- Vendor master changes require IT ticket — average 9 days turnaround.
- Wants: unified catalog, delegated authority matrix by category, supplier portal for ASN.

ACCOUNTS PAYABLE (Priya Sharma)
- Bangalore team handles data entry; Chicago team handles exceptions and accruals.
- Kofax OCR accuracy ~87% on first pass; line-item tables fail frequently on freight invoices.
- Biggest exception drivers: (1) price variance, (2) quantity mismatch, (3) missing GR, (4) wrong tax code.
- Peak volume: days 3–7 after month-end — overtime required.
- Quote: "We spend more time chasing requesters for receipts than actually matching."

WAREHOUSE (Lisa Nguyen)
- GR posting delayed 2–3 days for rush production materials — causes invoice blocks.
- Barcode scanning deployed at 2 of 5 US plants; others use paper tally sheets.
- Summit Plastics (NetSuite) has no WM module — manual spreadsheet handoff to AP.

IT (Tom Bradley)
- S/4 migration phase 2 planned Q3 2026 for EU; Summit integration TBD.
- API available for Coupa-SAP but not enabled for receipt status feedback.
- Concerned about change saturation — "We cannot run three cutovers in parallel."
