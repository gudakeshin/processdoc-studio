## Purpose

This memo establishes the agreed baseline for the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) workstream and the target improvements that underpin the $4.2M transformation business case. All figures are sourced from the FY2025 AP operational dashboard and the January 2026 close pack unless noted otherwise. Currency is USD.

## Pain points driving the case

1. **Manual match exceptions (high severity)** — ~6.2 FTE of annual effort. Root causes: price variance, missing goods receipt, quantity mismatch, and tax-coding errors.
2. **Fragmented systems (high severity)** — three ERP instances (NWM-US S/4HANA, NWM-EU ECC, Summit NetSuite), only partial Coupa integration, and manual Summit handoffs.
3. **Vendor master quality (medium severity)** — estimated 4% duplicate rate (source: MDG audit, November 2025).
4. **Receipt discipline (medium severity)** — average goods-receipt delay of 2.5 days; 3 of 5 US plants still lack barcode scanning.
5. **SOX segregation of duties (high severity)** — 11 open conflicts in Summit NetSuite, remediation in progress.

## Investment and timeline

- **Approved budget:** $4.2M over an 18-month program.
- **Wave 1:** P2P, selected because it touches ~60% of monthly transaction volume.
- **Primary outcome target:** reduce close from T+8 to T+5 while improving SOX readiness.

## Recommendation

Endorse the baseline above as the official measurement starting point and approve quarterly tracking of these eight metrics at the steering committee. Benefits realization should be reported against the target column with explicit source citations for each updated figure.
