# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process synthesizes evidence and insights into a coherent, compelling narrative that communicates key findings, risks, and recommendations to stakeholders. The Process Owner initiates narrative development to translate complex information into actionable business intelligence. The output serves senior leadership and decision-makers who require concise, evidence-grounded summaries.

## Key Activities

- **Build a narrative** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Synthesizes evidence into coherent narrative; owns narrative quality and stakeholder alignment.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Define narrative scope and target audience before synthesis begins.
2. Establish evidence validation criteria to ensure all claims are grounded in available data.
3. Schedule stakeholder review gate to confirm narrative alignment with business priorities.
4. Document narrative template and reusable structure for future iterations.
