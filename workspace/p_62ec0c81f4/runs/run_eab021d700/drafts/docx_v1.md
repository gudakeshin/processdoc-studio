# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process produces evidence-grounded executive narratives that synthesize process findings, risks, and prioritized actions for senior business audiences. The Process Owner initiates narrative development following process analysis and applies quality assurance remediation to ensure all claims are properly sourced and comply with brand standards. The output delivers a concise, actionable briefing document that communicates process purpose, activity flow, and recommended next steps within a 3-minute read time.

## Key Activities

- **Build narrative** — Process Owner creates initial draft synthesizing process evidence
- **Apply QA remediation** — Process Owner addresses structural and compliance failures identified in review

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for narrative creation, QA remediation application, and ensuring all claims are evidence-grounded and compliant with guardrail standards.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Establish a pre-submission checklist requiring explicit source attribution for all quantitative claims and benchmark references before QA review.
2. Implement a two-stage validation gate: structural compliance (outline, section order, word count) followed by guardrail compliance (hedging language, brand tone, unsupported claims).
3. Document the specific guardrail failures encountered in this cycle and create targeted remediation templates for future narrative builds.
4. Assign a secondary reviewer to verify cross-references between narrative claims and source ProcessModel data before final delivery.
