# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process synthesizes evidence-grounded narratives that communicate process purpose, activity flow, risks, and prioritized actions to senior business audiences. The Process Owner initiates narrative development to support executive decision-making and stakeholder alignment. The output delivers concise, actionable briefings that translate operational complexity into strategic clarity.

## Key Activities

- **Synthesize evidence-grounded narrative** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Synthesizes evidence-grounded narratives and delivers executive briefings.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Define narrative scope and target audience alignment criteria within 5 days.
2. Establish evidence sources and validation standards for narrative claims.
3. Create narrative template with required sections and tone guidelines.
4. Schedule monthly narrative review cycle with stakeholder feedback loop.
