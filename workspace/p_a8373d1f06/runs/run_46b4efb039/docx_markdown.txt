# Executive Briefing: Process Narrative Development

## Overview

This briefing addresses a critical structural gap in the current ProcessModel and outlines the path forward to deliver a valid executive briefing on your business process.

## Issue Identification

The ProcessModel currently describes a **meta-process** rather than an operational business workflow:

- **Single Activity**: "Build a narrative" with no sub-steps, decision points, or role transitions
- **Limited Roles**: Process Owner only—no cross-functional accountability
- **Missing Operational Detail**: No defined inputs, outputs, tools, timelines, or success metrics
- **QA Flags**: Metadata indicates `qa_outcome: fail` and `guardrail_outcome: fail` with remediation applied but unresolved

This structure prevents generation of a credible executive briefing, as doing so would require fabricating business context, activities, and risks absent from the model.

## Requirements for Valid Executive Briefing

A complete briefing requires:

| Element | Current State | Required |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| **Business Outcome** | Undefined | Clear value proposition |
| **Key Activities** | 1 step | ≥6 distinct steps with ownership |
| **Roles & Accountability** | 1 role | ≥2 roles with mandates |
| **Decision Points & Controls** | None | Risk-based decision branches |
| **Next Actions** | None | Specific, role-assigned improvements |

## Path Forward

**Please provide one of the following:**

### Option A: Real Business Process
Submit a ProcessModel with ≥3 steps, ≥2 roles, and decision branches (e.g., client onboarding, procurement, compliance workflow)

### Option B: Meta-Process Briefing
Clarify that this documents the **narrative-building process itself**, reframing the briefing to cover:
- Narrative synthesis from process evidence
- Quality gates and guardrail compliance
- Remediation workflows for QA failures
- Stakeholder review and sign-off procedures

### Option C: Parent Process Context
Provide the actual business process that "Build a Narrative" supports (e.g., "Customer Onboarding" where narrative synthesis is one step within a larger workflow)

## Next Steps

Reply with your preferred option and supporting ProcessModel details. This will enable delivery of a rigorous, evidence-based executive briefing aligned with your business objectives.
