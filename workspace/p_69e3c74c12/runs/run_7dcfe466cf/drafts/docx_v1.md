# Complete run

## Purpose

This process ensures systematic execution and completion of operational runs. It establishes a clear mandate for the Process Owner to oversee all activities required to bring a run to successful conclusion, maintaining consistency and accountability throughout the workflow.

## Scope

This procedure covers all activities necessary to complete a run from initiation through final execution. The Process Owner is responsible for managing all aspects of the run lifecycle within their designated operational domain.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for executing and completing the run, ensuring all required activities are performed and documented according to established standards.
