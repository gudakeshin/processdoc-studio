# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process synthesizes evidence-grounded narratives that communicate process purpose, activity flow, risks, and prioritized next actions to senior business audiences. The Process Owner initiates narrative development to translate operational complexity into concise executive briefings. This enables leadership to make informed decisions based on clear, actionable process intelligence.

## Key Activities

- **Synthesize evidence-grounded narrative** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for synthesizing evidence-grounded narratives and delivering executive briefings.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Define narrative scope and stakeholder audience with executive sponsor within 7 days.
2. Establish evidence sources and validation criteria for narrative claims.
3. Schedule monthly narrative review cycles to track process changes and risks.
4. Assign backup Process Owner to ensure continuity of narrative delivery.
