# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process synthesizes evidence-grounded executive narratives that communicate process purpose, activity flow, risks, and prioritized next actions to senior business audiences. The Process Owner initiates narrative generation based on stakeholder requirements and project context. The output delivers a concise, structured briefing that enables informed decision-making by translating complex operational details into actionable executive summaries.

## Key Activities

- **Build narrative structure** — Process Owner
- **Validate narrative against evidence** — Process Owner
- **Apply QA remediation** — Process Owner
- **Address guardrail compliance failures** — Process Owner
- **Finalize executive briefing** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for narrative generation, quality assurance, guardrail compliance, and delivery of evidence-grounded executive briefings to stakeholders.

## Key Risks and Controls

**Risk**: QA validation failures prevent narrative completion and delay stakeholder delivery. | **Mitigation**: Implement mandatory QA remediation protocol; document all failures and corrective actions before final release.

**Risk**: Guardrail compliance failures introduce non-compliant content into executive briefings. | **Mitigation**: Establish pre-delivery guardrail audit; require Process Owner sign-off on compliance status before distribution.

**Risk**: Single-role process creates bottleneck if Process Owner is unavailable. | **Mitigation**: Cross-train backup resource; document narrative-building standards and decision criteria in process runbook.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Resolve outstanding QA remediation items and document corrective actions taken to address the failed QA outcome.

2. Conduct guardrail compliance audit to identify root causes of guardrail failure and implement preventive controls.

3. Establish narrative quality checklist covering evidence grounding, stakeholder alignment, and compliance gates before Process Owner sign-off.

4. Schedule process review with stakeholders to confirm narrative structure meets executive audience expectations and business requirements.
