# Process Documentation: Complete Run

## Purpose

The Complete Run process establishes the operational framework for executing a comprehensive workflow cycle that integrates quality assurance validation, remediation protocols, and guardrail compliance verification. This process exists to ensure that all deliverables meet established quality standards, that identified deficiencies are systematically addressed through documented remediation activities, and that governance controls are applied consistently throughout the execution lifecycle. By formalizing the complete run procedure, the organization maintains accountability, traceability, and continuous improvement in process execution and output quality.

## Scope

The Complete Run process encompasses the full cycle of workflow execution, including initial setup, quality assurance evaluation, remediation of identified gaps, and guardrail compliance assessment. This process applies to all deliverables and outputs that require formal quality validation and governance oversight. The scope includes coordination with the Process Owner role, documentation of QA outcomes and remediation actions, and verification that all guardrail requirements have been satisfied before final completion. Systems and tools involved in execution, validation, and documentation are integrated throughout this process to maintain consistency and auditability.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Process Owner**: The Process Owner holds primary accountability for executing the complete run workflow, managing the quality assurance evaluation cycle, implementing remediation actions in response to QA findings, and ensuring that all guardrail compliance requirements are met before process completion. The Process Owner is responsible for documenting outcomes, maintaining audit trails, and escalating any unresolved issues that prevent successful process closure.

## Procedure

1. **Execute quality assurance evaluation**: The Process Owner initiates the complete run by conducting a comprehensive quality assurance evaluation of all deliverables and outputs. This step involves reviewing work products against established quality standards, documenting findings, and recording the QA outcome. If the QA evaluation results in a failed outcome, the Process Owner documents the specific deficiencies identified and prepares for remediation activities in the next step.

2. **Implement remediation and verify guardrail compliance**: Following QA evaluation, the Process Owner implements remediation actions to address any identified deficiencies. Once remediation is complete, the Process Owner verifies guardrail compliance status by assessing whether all governance and control requirements have been satisfied. If guardrail evaluation results in a failed outcome, the Process Owner documents the guardrail failure, initiates appropriate corrective actions, and ensures all requirements are resolved before finalizing the complete run cycle.
