# Process Documentation: Complete Run

## Purpose

This procedure documents the completion of a process run cycle, including the execution of quality assurance validation and remediation of identified issues. The process ensures that all deliverables meet established quality standards and that any guardrail violations are systematically addressed before final sign-off. This process is essential for maintaining consistency, compliance, and stakeholder confidence in the output.

## Scope

This procedure covers the activities required to complete a process run from initial execution through quality assurance validation and remediation. It includes the assessment of QA outcomes, application of remediation steps when failures are detected, and resolution of guardrail violations. The scope encompasses all documentation, validation checks, and corrective actions necessary to bring the process run to completion status.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for executing the complete run, managing quality assurance validation, applying remediation measures when QA outcomes fail, and addressing any guardrail violations. The Process Owner ensures all steps are completed in sequence and that the final deliverable meets all established quality and compliance standards.

## Procedure

1. Complete run — The Process Owner executes the complete run cycle. This step encompasses the full process execution, including all required activities, validations, and quality checks. The Process Owner monitors progress and ensures all inputs are properly processed and all outputs are generated according to specifications. Upon completion of the initial run, the Process Owner documents the outcome and prepares for quality assurance review.
