# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process produces a structured narrative that synthesizes evidence and communicates key findings to stakeholders. The Process Owner initiates narrative development to translate complex information into clear, actionable business insights. The output serves internal and external audiences requiring concise, evidence-grounded communication.

## Key Activities

- **Build a narrative** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for developing, structuring, and delivering the narrative output.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Define narrative scope, audience, and success criteria with stakeholders.
2. Establish evidence sources and validation gates before narrative finalization.
3. Schedule review cycle with key decision-makers to confirm alignment.
4. Document narrative template and reusable components for future iterations.
