# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process synthesizes evidence-grounded narratives that communicate process purpose, activity flow, risks, and prioritized actions to senior business audiences. The Process Owner initiates narrative development to deliver concise executive briefings. Stakeholders benefit from clear, actionable process summaries that support decision-making and strategic alignment.

## Key Activities

- **Synthesize evidence-grounded narrative** — Process Owner
- **Communicate process purpose and outcomes** — Process Owner
- **Identify and document key risks** — Process Owner
- **Prioritize next actions for stakeholders** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Synthesizes evidence-grounded narratives; communicates process purpose, risks, and prioritized actions to senior audiences.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Define narrative scope and target audience stakeholder profile for each briefing.
2. Establish evidence sources and validation criteria for claims in narratives.
3. Create narrative template with required sections for consistency across briefings.
4. Schedule monthly narrative review with executive stakeholders to refine approach.
