# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process produces a structured narrative output that synthesizes information into a coherent, business-focused summary. The Process Owner initiates and executes the narrative building activity, delivering a completed narrative document. This output serves stakeholders requiring clear, evidence-grounded communication of process insights and strategic recommendations.

## Key Activities

- **Build a narrative** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for building and delivering the narrative output.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Define narrative scope and audience requirements before initiating the build activity.
2. Establish quality criteria and review checkpoints for narrative validation.
3. Schedule stakeholder review sessions to confirm narrative alignment with business objectives.
4. Document narrative templates and reusable frameworks for future builds.
