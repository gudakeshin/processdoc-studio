# Build a Narrative — Executive Briefing

## What This Process Does

This process enables the Process Owner to synthesize evidence-grounded narratives that communicate process purpose, activity flow, risks, and prioritized actions to senior stakeholders. The narrative output serves as an executive briefing tool, translating operational complexity into concise, actionable insights for decision-makers. Initiation occurs when stakeholders require a structured summary of a business process.

## Key Activities

- **Synthesize narrative content** — Process Owner
- **Document process purpose and outcomes** — Process Owner
- **Identify and communicate key risks** — Process Owner
- **Prioritize next actions** — Process Owner

## Roles and Accountability

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for synthesizing evidence-grounded narratives, documenting process flows, identifying risks, and recommending prioritized next actions for executive audiences.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. Define narrative scope and target audience stakeholder profile within 5 business days.
2. Establish evidence sources and validation criteria for narrative claims by end of week.
3. Create narrative template with required sections (purpose, activities, risks, actions) for consistency.
4. Schedule stakeholder review gate before finalizing narrative output for distribution.
