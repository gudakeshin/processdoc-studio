# Complete Run

## Purpose

The Complete Run process ensures that all deliverables meet quality and compliance standards before final release. This process establishes a structured approach to validating work outputs, identifying deficiencies through quality assurance and guardrail assessments, and implementing corrective actions. By systematically evaluating each run against defined criteria, the organization maintains consistency, reduces rework, and delivers reliable outputs to stakeholders.

## Scope

This process covers the final validation phase of any run, including user intent confirmation, quality assurance (QA) assessment, remediation of QA failures, and guardrail compliance evaluation. The scope encompasses all activities performed by the Process Owner to verify that outputs meet established standards before completion. This process applies to all runs requiring formal sign-off and does not include initial development or design phases.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Process Owner**: Responsible for updating user intent, executing QA and guardrail assessments, and applying remediation measures to address identified failures. The Process Owner ensures all steps are completed in sequence and that outputs meet quality standards before the run is marked complete.

## Procedure

1. Update user intent to complete run. The Process Owner reviews and confirms the current user intent, ensuring that the scope and objectives of the run remain aligned with stakeholder expectations. This step validates that no changes to requirements have occurred since the run began and documents the final intent for the record.

2. Execute QA assessment. The Process Owner conducts a comprehensive quality assurance evaluation of all deliverables against established quality criteria. This assessment identifies any defects, inconsistencies, or gaps in the output. The QA outcome is documented and determines whether the run can proceed or requires remediation.

3. Apply QA remediation. If the QA assessment results in a failure, the Process Owner implements corrective actions to address identified issues. This step involves analyzing the root causes of failures and applying targeted fixes to bring outputs into compliance with quality standards. Remediation is documented and tracked for audit purposes.

4. Execute guardrail assessment. The Process Owner performs a final guardrail compliance check to ensure that all outputs adhere to organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and established guardrails. This assessment serves as a final control gate before the run is marked complete and outputs are released.
