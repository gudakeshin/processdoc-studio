# CFO Metrics — Decision Tree

Finance leaders track specific metrics tied to their business model and strategy. Pick the right metrics to show you understand their priorities.

---

## Decision tree: Match engagement type to metrics

### Close Acceleration / Month-End Optimization

**Core metrics:**
- **Close calendar (days)** — Current vs. industry benchmark vs. target
  - Benchmark: 4-5 days (best-in-class), 8-10 days (mature), 12+ days (lagging)
  - Drivers: GL reconciliation time, consolidation, audit/compliance tasks
  
- **Critical path task duration (days)** — Tasks that block downstream processes
  - Example: "GL reconciliation is currently 5 of 12 close days; we believe we can reduce to 2 days"
  
- **Manual GL reconciliation % of close effort** — Currently X%, target Y%
  - Typical: 35-45% of close effort is GL reconciliation
  - Best practice: <10%
  
- **Manual journal entry volume** — Count per month, as % of total transactions
  - Typical: 5,000-10,000 manual entries per month
  - Opportunity: Automate recurring entries (60% of manual volume)

**Secondary metrics:**
- SOX control findings related to close (count, severity)
- Manual rework rate (% of entries requiring correction)
- Audit time required (hours, cost)
- Time to produce final P&L (hours after close)

---

### FP&A Transformation / Planning & Forecasting

**Core metrics:**
- **Forecast accuracy (%)** — Variance from actual to forecast
  - Current: ±6% to ±10% typical
  - Best practice: ±2% to ±3%
  - Calculation: Average |Forecast - Actual| / Actual
  
- **Forecast cycle time (weeks)** — Time from kick-off to results
  - Current: 4-6 weeks typical
  - Best practice: 2-3 weeks
  - Drivers: Data collection, consolidation, governance reviews
  
- **Scenario modeling turnaround (days)** — Time to model "what-if" scenario
  - Current: 3-5 days typical (requires manual data pulls, spreadsheet rework)
  - Target: 1 day or less
  
- **Budget/plan governance meetings (count, duration)** — How many reviews block the process
  - Current: 8-12 review cycles typical
  - Target: 4-6 cycles

**Secondary metrics:**
- Variance explanation time (how long to explain forecast misses)
- Planning headcount (FTE dedicated to budget/forecast)
- Data refresh frequency (monthly, quarterly, rolling 13-week)
- Scenario depth (e.g., "can model revenue, COGS, and G&A independently" vs. "only top-line scenarios")

---

### GBS / Operating Model Transformation

**Core metrics:**
- **Finance cost as % of revenue** — Total finance spend / revenue
  - Current: 0.8-1.2% typical
  - Best practice: 0.4-0.6%
  - Benchmark: Industry, peer group, internal target
  
- **Headcount per $B revenue (FTE)** — Finance team size relative to scale
  - Current: 40-50 FTE per $B typical
  - Best practice: 20-25 FTE per $B
  
- **GBS penetration (%)** — % of transactional finance work done in shared services
  - Current: 20-40% typical
  - Target: 60-80%
  
- **Finance headcount by category (%)**
  - Transactional (AR/AP/GL/Cash): Target <25% (automation/GBS candidate)
  - Operational (period-end, reporting): Target 40-50%
  - Analytical (FP&A, planning): Target 15-20%
  - Leadership/governance: Target 5-10%

**Secondary metrics:**
- Process cost per transaction (AR, AP, GL)
- Automation rate (% of transactional tasks automated)
- Off-shoring penetration (if applicable)
- Process standardization (# of processes across regions)
- Finance system consolidation (# of systems vs. target)

---

### Working Capital Optimization

**Core metrics:**
- **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)** — How long to collect cash after invoice
  - Formula: (Accounts Receivable / Revenue) × Days
  - Current: 45-60 days typical
  - Target: 30-40 days
  - Impact: 10-day improvement = ~$5M-$15M cash release (varies by revenue)
  
- **Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)** — How long before you pay suppliers
  - Formula: (Accounts Payable / COGS) × Days
  - Current: 35-45 days typical
  - Target: 50-60 days (if relationships allow)
  - Impact: 10-day improvement = ~$3M-$10M cash release
  
- **Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO)** — How long inventory sits before sale
  - Formula: (Inventory / COGS) × Days
  - Current: 40-60 days typical (varies by industry)
  - Target: 30-40 days
  
- **Cash-to-cash cycle (days)** — DSO + DIO - DPO
  - Current: 60-90 days typical
  - Target: 30-50 days
  - Impact: Every 1 day improvement = ~$500K-$2M cash release (varies)

**Secondary metrics:**
- Early pay discounts captured (%)
- Inventory write-off / obsolescence (%)
- Bad debt reserve as % of AR
- Supplier payment term compliance

---

### Treasury / Cash Management Transformation

**Core metrics:**
- **Cash visibility** — How frequently does CFO see true cash position?
  - Current: End-of-day or next-morning reports typical
  - Target: Intraday visibility (EOD at minimum, real-time ideally)
  
- **Banking relationship optimization** — # of bank accounts, cross-border consolidation
  - Current: 30-50+ accounts across regions typical
  - Target: Consolidated to 10-15 regional hubs
  
- **Payment processing time** — % of payments processed automatically vs. manually
  - Current: 60% manual (exception handling) typical
  - Target: 85%+ automated
  
- **Working capital financing costs** — Interest expense, fees on short-term credit
  - Current: Compare to benchmark for improved cash management
  - Target: Reduce through better DSO/DPO management

**Secondary metrics:**
- Counterparty credit risk exposure (concentration)
- FX hedging effectiveness (% of exposure hedged)
- Liquidity forecasting accuracy

## Selecting metrics: Template

**If the engagement is close/consolidation:**
→ Use: Close calendar, GL reconciliation %, manual journal %, critical path tasks, SOX findings, time to P&L

**If the engagement is FP&A/planning:**
→ Use: Forecast accuracy, forecast cycle time, scenario turnaround, # of plan cycles, planning headcount

**If the engagement is GBS/operating model:**
→ Use: Finance cost %, headcount per $B, GBS penetration, transactional FTE %, system consolidation

**If the engagement is working capital:**
→ Use: DSO, DPO, DIO, cash-to-cash cycle, cash impact ($M)

**If the engagement is treasury/cash management:**
→ Use: Cash visibility (frequency), # of bank accounts, % automated payments, working capital cost

**Always include a risk metric:**
→ SOX control findings, rework rate, audit issues, incident/error frequency, or staff retention

## Anchoring with benchmarks

If actual client metrics are unknown, use illustrative ranges and label clearly.

### Format
"[Illustrative] Industry benchmark for close calendar is 4-5 days for best-in-class companies of your scale. You're currently at 12 days, representing an 8-day opportunity."

### Common benchmark sources
- **Deloitte Finance Operating Model Benchmark** (internal)
- **CFO Innovation Council** (external, membership-based)
- **Hackett Group** (external research firm)
- **Institute of Management Accountants (IMA)** (peer benchmarks)
- **Industry consortiums** (e.g., SAP User Group, Oracle User Community)

### Important caveat
Never cite "best-in-class" without context. "Best-in-class" for a 2-person finance team looks different from a 200-person organization. Always qualify: "for companies of your scale in your industry."

## Red flags: Metrics to avoid

**Avoid vanity metrics:**
- ❌ "Improved automation" (automation of what? By how much?)
- ❌ "Enhanced visibility" (visibility into what? Who uses it?)
- ❌ "Streamlined processes" (which processes? Measured how?)

**Avoid metrics without a CFO goal:**
- ❌ "Reduced system count from 12 to 8" (unless consolidation was the goal)
- ❌ "Achieved 95% data quality" (unless accuracy was the pain point)
- ❌ "Deployed real-time reporting" (unless decision speed was the constraint)

**Avoid metrics without business impact:**
- ❌ "Implemented machine learning" (for what outcome?)
- ❌ "Migrated to cloud" (what problem did this solve?)
- ❌ "Trained 100 people" (what changed in their work as a result?)

Every metric should answer: "Why does the CFO care about this?"
