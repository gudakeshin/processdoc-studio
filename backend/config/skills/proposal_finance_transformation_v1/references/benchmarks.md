# Benchmarks — Illustrative Ranges

Use these ranges when client-specific metrics are not available. Always label as [illustrative] and qualify by company size and industry.

---

## Close acceleration benchmarks

| Metric | Lagging | Mature | Best-in-class |
|--------|---------|--------|----------------|
| **Close calendar (workdays)** | 12-15 | 8-10 | 3-5 |
| **GL reconciliation as % of close** | 40-50% | 25-30% | <5% |
| **Manual journal entries per month** | 8,000-12,000 | 3,000-5,000 | <500 |
| **% of entries requiring rework** | 8-12% | 2-3% | <1% |
| **Time to P&L (hours after close)** | 8-16 | 4-6 | <2 |
| **# of approval sign-offs in close** | 8-12 | 4-6 | 1-2 |
| **SAP/Oracle GL reconciliation automation %** | 10-20% | 40-60% | 90%+ |

**Context:**
- Best-in-class assumes process discipline + appropriate technology
- Does NOT assume full ERP implementation; process/org are bigger levers (70% of value)
- Lagging → Mature (typical transformation): 3-4 months, 1-2 FTE months effort
- Mature → Best-in-class (additional modernization): 2-3 months, 0.5-1 FTE months effort

## Working capital benchmarks

| Metric | Lagging | Median | Best-in-class |
|--------|---------|--------|----------------|
| **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)** | 55-70 | 40-50 | 25-35 |
| **Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)** | 30-40 | 45-55 | 60-75 |
| **Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO)** | 60-90 | 45-60 | 30-40 |
| **Cash-to-cash cycle (days)** | 80-120 | 40-60 | 10-30 |
| **% of early pay discounts captured** | 40-60% | 70-85% | 90%+ |
| **Days sales in cash (DSC)** | 80-100 | 50-70 | 20-40 |

**Context:**
- DSO varies by industry (B2B higher, retail lower)
- DPO improvement requires supplier relationship management (not just process)
- Cash impact: Each 1-day DSO improvement = ~$500K-$2M cash release (varies by revenue)
- DSC metric (alternative to DSO) accounts for both receivables and inventory

**Examples by industry:**
- **Manufacturing**: DSO 45-55, DPO 45-55, DIO 60-90 → typical C2C 60-90 days
- **Distribution**: DSO 40-50, DPO 50-60, DIO 45-60 → typical C2C 35-50 days
- **B2B SaaS**: DSO 60-90 (annual contracts, longer collection), DPO 40-50 → typical C2C 70-100 days

## How to use these benchmarks in proposals

### Template 1: Single metric comparison

"[Illustrative] Best-in-class close calendar for companies of your scale in manufacturing is 4-5 days. You're currently at 12 days, representing an 8-day opportunity that translates to ~$2M annual value through CFO decision speed and working capital optimization."

### Template 2: Multi-metric assessment

"Your finance function metrics benchmark as follows:

| Metric | Current | Mature | Best-in-class |
|--------|---------|--------|---|
| Close calendar (days) | 12 | 8-10 | 3-5 |
| GL recon % of close | 45% | 25-30% | <5% |
| FTE per $B revenue | 45 | 25-35 | 15-20 |

This suggests the largest opportunities are in close acceleration (8-day reduction) and organizational efficiency (30% FTE reduction through process/automation)."

### Template 3: Waterfall impact

"Working capital transformation opportunity across three levers:

1. **DSO improvement** (55 days → 40 days) = $12M cash release
2. **DPO improvement** (40 days → 50 days) = $8M cash release
3. **DIO optimization** (80 days → 65 days) = $5M cash release
**Total: $25M cash impact from working capital optimization**"

## Important caveats

**Use ranges, not point estimates.** No two companies are identical. "Best-in-class close is 4-5 days" is better than "4 days" because it acknowledges variation.

**Qualify by context.** Always note:
- "For mid-market manufacturers of your scale"
- "In your industry"
- "With your technology footprint"

**Don't cherry-pick benchmarks.** Don't present only "lagging" benchmarks to inflate opportunity. Show the full range (lagging → mature → best-in-class) so the client can gauge realistic targets.

**Source your benchmarks.** If you cite them, be prepared to reference:
- Deloitte FO Benchmark (internal)
- CFO Innovation Council (external, membership-based)
- Hackett Group (external research)
- Industry consortium (if applicable)

**Update benchmarks regularly.** Technology and process practices evolve. Revisit benchmarks annually.
