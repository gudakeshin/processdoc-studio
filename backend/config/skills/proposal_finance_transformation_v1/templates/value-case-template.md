# Value Case Template — Quantified Benefits Format

Use this template to articulate value across multiple levers. One lever (cost only) is weak; 3+ levers (cost + working capital + risk) is strong.

---

## Value Case Structure

### Template 1: Three-Lever Value Case (Close Acceleration)

**Lever 1: Close Speed Impact**
- CFO gains decision-making time (6 days earlier)
- Quantification:
  - Current: P&L available Day 12 of month
  - Future: P&L available Day 6 of month
  - Benefit: Faster capital allocation decisions, improved working capital decisions
  - Annual value: $1.2M (estimated from improved decision timeliness)

**Lever 2: Operational Efficiency**
- GL reconciliation automation reduces FTE required
- Quantification:
  - Current: GL reconciliation is 45% of close effort = 2.5 FTE equivalent
  - Future: GL reconciliation is 5% of close effort = 0.3 FTE equivalent
  - FTE savings: 2.2 FTE redirected to higher-value work
  - Annual value: $300K (2.2 FTE @ $135K average loaded cost)

**Lever 3: Risk & Compliance**
- SAP control findings reduced through automation
- Quantification:
  - Current: 6 SAP-related findings per year
  - Future: <2 findings per year (4-finding reduction)
  - Benefit: Lower audit cost, reduced remediation effort, improved internal control rating
  - Annual value: $150K (audit cost reduction $100K + compliance savings $50K)

**Lever 4 (Optional): Working Capital**
- Faster close enables faster decision-making on cash/payables
- Quantification:
  - Current: DPO management delayed due to late close visibility
  - Future: Can implement DPO optimization immediately
  - Benefit: Opportunity to extend DPO by 2-3 days
  - Annual value: $500K-$1M (for typical $500M-$1B revenue company)

**TOTAL VALUE: $2.15M-$2.65M annually**

---

### Template 2: GBS Value Case (Multi-Lever)

**Lever 1: Cost Reduction (FTE)**
- Centralize transactional finance to shared services
- Quantification:
  - Current: Finance cost 0.9% of revenue = $45M for $5B company
  - Future: Finance cost 0.6% of revenue (industry benchmark)
  - Cost reduction: 0.3% of revenue = $15M annually
  - Achievable through: 25% FTE reduction + automation + GBS labor arbitrage

**Lever 2: Quality & Compliance**
- Centralized processes reduce variance; standardization improves controls
- Quantification:
  - Current: 6 financial statement adjustment findings, 30% manual controls
  - Future: 0-2 findings, 70% automated controls
  - Benefit: Reduced audit findings, faster audit closeout
  - Annual value: $200K (audit time savings, compliance cost reduction)

**Lever 3: Scalability**
- GBS model enables growth without proportional cost growth
- Quantification:
  - As company grows 10%, legacy finance grows 10% (cost increases)
  - GBS model enables 10% growth with 5% finance cost increase (semi-fixed leverage)
  - Over 3 years with projected 8% annual growth: $2M+ incremental savings
  - Year 1 impact: $300K (partial year benefit)

**Lever 4: Capability Expansion**
- Redeployed FTE can focus on strategic finance (FP&A, analytics)
- Quantification:
  - Current: 5 FTE in analytical work (limited due to transactional load)
  - Future: 12 FTE in analytical work (freed from GBS transition)
  - Benefit: Better planning, faster analysis, improved decision support
  - Annual value: Difficult to quantify directly; estimate $500K-$1M in decision value

**TOTAL VALUE: $16M+ in Year 1 (cost + quality + scalability), $18M+ run-rate**

### Template 3: FP&A Value Case (Decision Quality)

**Lever 1: Forecast Accuracy**
- Improved forecast models reduce variance
- Quantification:
  - Current: ±8% forecast variance
  - Future: ±3% forecast variance
  - Benefit: Fewer surprises; better capital allocation decisions
  - Annual value: Difficult to quantify directly (opportunity cost of missed decisions)
  - Estimate: $2M-$5M (from better capital allocation, reduced buffer inventory)

**Lever 2: Planning Cycle Speed**
- Faster planning cycle enables more iterations
- Quantification:
  - Current: Budget cycle takes 6 weeks; only 2 revisions per year
  - Future: Budget cycle takes 3 weeks; enables 6 revisions per year
  - Benefit: More agile planning; better response to market changes
  - Annual value: $500K-$1M (estimated from improved plan responsiveness)

**Lever 3: Scenario Capability**
- Enhanced planning enables more sophisticated scenario analysis
- Quantification:
  - Current: 3-5 scenarios per planning cycle (limited by modeling time)
  - Future: 20+ scenarios enabled by automated modeling
  - Benefit: Better risk quantification; improved strategic decision-making
  - Annual value: $1M-$3M (estimated from better risk-adjusted decisions)

**Lever 4: Staffing Efficiency**
- Automation reduces manual data pulling and consolidation
- Quantification:
  - Current: 40% of FP&A team time on data consolidation
  - Future: 10% of team time on data consolidation
  - FTE savings: 1.5 FTE redirected to analytics and decision support
  - Annual value: $150K-$200K (FTE cost savings)

**TOTAL VALUE: $3.65M-$5.2M annually (heavily dependent on capital allocation decisions improved)**

## How to estimate value for each lever

### Close Acceleration (Close Speed Value)

**If you can quantify:**
- Faster working capital decisions enabled by earlier P&L
- DSO/DPO management improvements from faster visibility
- Cost of rework due to late close data (e.g., finance team overtime)

**Formula:**
- Revenue × 0.3% = typical annual value of close acceleration
- Varies by company (tech companies see less value from close speed than capital-intensive)

**Range:** $500K-$2M for typical $500M-$2B company

---

### GL Automation / Operational Efficiency

**Formula:**
- Current time spent on GL reconciliation (hours/month) × labor cost ($/hour) × 12 months
- Discount by % achievable through automation (e.g., 40% of effort automated, not 90%)

**Example:**
- GL recon: 200 hours/month × $75/hour = $15K/month = $180K/year
- Automation saves 60% of effort (realistic after pilot): $180K × 60% = $108K/year

**Range:** $100K-$500K depending on company size and current manual effort

---

### Risk / Compliance Value

**Components:**
- Audit cost reduction: (# findings × cost to remediate) × reduction in findings
  - Cost per audit finding: $10K-$50K (varies by severity, industry, audit firm)
  - Example: 6 findings × $20K × 67% reduction (from 6 to 2) = $80K/year

- Compliance cost: Reduced headcount for control testing, documentation
  - Estimate: $0.5-2 FTE for compliance/audit prep in large organizations
  - Cost: $50K-$300K/year

**Range:** $100K-$500K depending on current control maturity

---

### Working Capital Value

**Formula:**
- (DSO improvement in days × Daily revenue) + (DPO improvement in days × Daily COGS) + (DIO improvement in days × Daily COGS)
- Example: $1B revenue company
  - DSO improvement: 5 days × ($1B/365) = $13.7M cash release
  - DPO improvement: 5 days × ($600M COGS/365) = $8.2M cash use reduction
  - Total cash impact: $21.9M (one-time), or 5% of that annually ($1.1M) if annualized

**Important:** Working capital value is typically one-time cash release, not ongoing annual value. Annualize conservatively (5-10% of cash release).

**Range:** $500K-$5M+ depending on company size and working capital improvement opportunity

---

### GBS Cost Value

**Formula:**
- Current finance cost (% of revenue × revenue) - Future finance cost (% of revenue × revenue)
- Example: $5B revenue
  - Current: 0.9% × $5B = $45M
  - Future: 0.6% × $5B = $30M
  - Annual savings: $15M
  - Net of GBS startup costs: GBS setup $2M, so Year 1 net benefit = $13M

**Key:** GBS value calculation is straightforward but requires commitment to staffing plan.

**Range:** $5M-$25M for large organizations; $1M-$5M for mid-market

## What makes a strong value case

✅ **Strong:**
- Three or more value levers (not just cost)
- Quantified in dollars (not percentages or "significant")
- Credible (backed by industry benchmarks or client data)
- Achievable (conservative assumptions; accounts for execution risk)
- Sustainable (not one-time; run-rate benefits)

❌ **Weak:**
- Only cost reduction (no other levers)
- Unsubstantiated ("improved efficiency" without numbers)
- Over-optimistic (assumes 90% automation, impossible manual reductions)
- Misaligned (CFO doesn't care about metrics you're highlighting)

## Value realization timeline

Different levers realize value at different times:

| Lever | Realization Timeline |
|-------|----------------------|
| **Close speed** | Months 3-4 (after automation deployed) |
| **GL automation savings** | Months 2-3 (as automation ramp-up completes) |
| **Risk reduction** | Months 4-6 (measured at next audit cycle) |
| **Working capital** | Months 1-3 (cash benefit immediate if DPO extended) |
| **GBS cost savings** | Months 4-6 (ramp-up period, partial benefit) |

**Typical pattern:**
- Months 1-2: No financial benefit (investment phase)
- Months 3-4: Partial benefit (automation ramping; cost not fully realized)
- Months 5-6: Full benefit achieved and sustained
- Year 2+: Full run-rate benefit

## How to present value case in proposal

### Option 1: Waterfall chart
Shows each lever's contribution:
- Starting point (current cost or inefficiency)
- Each lever adds value (GL automation $300K, risk reduction $150K, etc.)
- Ending point (total value $2M+)

### Option 2: Table
Simple format showing lever, current, future, value:

| Lever | Current State | Future State | Annual Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Close calendar | 12 days | 6 days | $1.2M |
| GL automation | 45% effort, 2.5 FTE | 5% effort, 0.3 FTE | $300K |
| Risk findings | 6 findings/year | <2 findings/year | $150K |
| TOTAL | | | $1.65M |

### Option 3: Narrative
Paragraph format describing each lever:
"The $1.65M annual value comes from three sources: First, reducing close from 12 to 6 days enables CFO to make capital decisions 6 days earlier ($1.2M value). Second, GL reconciliation automation reduces the team from 2.5 to 0.3 FTE ($300K savings). Third, eliminating 4 SAP findings annually reduces audit cost ($150K)."

## Common mistakes to avoid

❌ **Stacking assumptions** — "If we automate GL (assumption 1) AND extend DPO (assumption 2) AND eliminate 2 approvals (assumption 3), we save $5M." Too many "ifs."

❌ **Assuming tech solves everything** — "New GL system will improve forecast accuracy by ±10%." Technology alone doesn't improve accuracy; process and discipline do.

❌ **Ignoring TCO** — Value of $3M vs. cost of $2M is only a 1.5x return. Be honest about costs (not just engagement fee, but internal effort, system costs, ongoing support).

❌ **One-time value as annual** — "Close acceleration saves 2 weeks of month-end overtime = $50K," but that's one-time. Ongoing annual value is $0.

❌ **Vague value drivers** — "Improved efficiency" or "enhanced visibility" without quantification.

✅ **Do this instead:**
- Quantify each lever separately
- Show how levers compound (don't double-count)
- Be conservative on assumptions
- Account for implementation costs and ramp-up period
- Clear about one-time vs. recurring value
