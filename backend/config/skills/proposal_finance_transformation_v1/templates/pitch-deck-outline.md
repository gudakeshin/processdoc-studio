## Slide Structure (12-18 slides, ~30-45 min presentation)

### Slides 1-2: Opener

**Slide 1: Title slide**
- Title: "Finance Close Acceleration Transformation"
- Subtitle: "Achieving 6-day close calendar and $2M+ annual value"
- Company logo
- Date
- "Confidential"

**Slide 2: Agenda**
- Problem: Where you are (12-day close, challenges)
- Opportunity: What's possible (6-day close, why it matters)
- Approach: How we'll get there (4-phase plan, timeline)
- Value: What you'll gain ($2M+ annually)
- Next steps: What happens now

---

### Slide 3: The Problem

**Slide 3: Current-state challenge** (narrative + data)
- Headline: "Your month-end close has extended from 8 days (2022) to 12 days (2024), limiting strategic decision-making"
- Current state (icons + numbers):
  - Close calendar: 12 workdays
  - GL reconciliation: 45% of close effort (manual)
  - Approval steps: 8 (some redundant)
  - Audit findings: 6 SAP-related

- Impact: "CFO can't make capital allocation decisions until Day 12; planning cycles are compressed; audit risk remains high"

**Visual:** Process flow showing 12-day timeline with bottlenecks highlighted

---

### Slide 4: Benchmark

**Slide 4: Opportunity gap** (benchmark comparison)
- Your company: 12 days (gap analysis)
- Industry best-in-class: 4-5 days
- Opportunity: 7-8 day reduction (67% improvement)

- Secondary benchmarks:
  - GL reconciliation: 45% of effort → best-in-class <5% (40-point opportunity)
  - Audit findings: 6 findings → best-in-class <1 (5-point reduction)

**Visual:** Bar chart showing current vs. benchmark; or "opportunity pyramid" showing size of each lever

---

### Slide 5: Root Cause

**Slide 5: Why is close taking 12 days?** (not tech, not "digital transformation")
- Process: 8 approval steps, sequential (not parallel) — 2 days of delay
- Organization: Close owner is 10% role; insufficient focus — rework and delays
- Data: 15% of GL accounts unmapped; subledger-to-GL reconciliation manual — 5 days
- Technology: GL reconciliation in Excel; limited automation — 3 days
- NOT a technology problem; 70% process/organization, 30% technology

**Visual:** 5-part pie chart showing root cause breakdown

---

### Slide 6: Our Hypothesis

**Slide 6: What we believe will move the needle** (crisp statement)
- Headline: "We believe you can achieve 6-day close in 4 months through process redesign and targeted automation, without full GL modernization"
- How:
  - Process redesign: Eliminate redundant approvals, enable parallel execution (2-day improvement)
  - GL reconciliation automation: Auto-post 90% of routine GL transactions (3-day improvement)
  - Data remediation: Fix unmapped accounts; enable automation (2-day improvement)

- Why realistic: "We've delivered this approach for 8+ comparable companies; 12-day → 6-day close is achievable in 4 months"

**Visual:** Box diagram showing "12 days → 9 days → 6 days" with each lever labeled

---

### Slides 7-9: Approach (3 slides)

**Slide 7: Phase 1 — Diagnose & Design (Weeks 1-4)**
- Assess current close process
- Analyze GL data quality and system architecture
- Design future-state process and GL reconciliation automation rules
- Plan client commitments and decision gates
- Deliverable: Design document + project plan
- Client commitment: Close owner 50%, GL manager 25%

**Visual:** Week-by-week timeline; key activities icons; deliverables checklist

---

**Slide 8: Phases 2-3 — Build & Pilot (Weeks 5-14)**
- Phase 2 (Weeks 5-10): Data remediation, system configuration, training prep
  - Deliverable: Remediated data, updated procedures, training materials
  - Client commitment: Close owner 75%, GL manager 50%
  
- Phase 3 (Weeks 11-14): Pilot close with new process, refine, full team training
  - Deliverable: Pilot results, refined procedures, training completion
  - Client commitment: Close owner 100%, full team in pilot
  - Decision gate: Pilot achieves 6 days? Proceed to full deployment

**Visual:** Timeline showing Phase 2 and 3 in parallel/sequence

---

**Slide 9: Phase 4 — Deploy & Transition (Weeks 15-17)**
- Execute full close with new process
- 24/7 support during first close
- Measure performance and validate benefits
- Transition to business-as-usual

- Total timeline: 17 weeks (4 months) to full 6-day capability

**Visual:** Week 15-17 timeline; "Go Live" milestone highlighted

## Presentation Tips

### Delivery approach
- **Slides 1-5** (5-7 min): Opener + problem setting
- **Slides 6-9** (8-10 min): Our hypothesis + approach
- **Slides 10-12** (8-10 min): Value + risks + proof
- **Slides 13-16** (8-10 min): Team + investment + next steps
- **Total: 30-40 min presentation + 5-10 min Q&A**

### Key talking points per slide

**Slide 3 (Problem):** "Your current 12-day close calendar is consistent with what we see across manufacturing companies at your scale that haven't optimized for close speed. The impact is real — your CFO can't make capital decisions until day 12, and your audit team is finding control gaps in your close process."

**Slide 6 (Hypothesis):** "This is not a technology problem that requires a $5M GL modernization. It's a process and organizational problem that we can solve in 4 months for $500K through disciplined process redesign and targeted automation. We've done this 8 times in the past 3 years."

**Slide 10 (Value):** "The $2.3M annual value comes from three places: first, close speed means your CFO makes decisions 6 days earlier (that's worth $1.2M in faster capital allocation decisions); second, we're eliminating 40% of GL effort through automation (that's $300K in FTE redirection); and third, we're resolving your SAP control findings (that's $150K in reduced audit cost)."

### Anticipate questions
- **"How realistic is 6-day close?"** → "For companies your size, 4-5 days is best-in-class. 6 days is aggressive but achievable in 4 months. We've done it 8 times."
- **"What if we don't have time during audit season?"** → "We phase the project around your Oct-Dec audit. Design in Q1, pilot in Q2-Q3, deploy in Q1 next year. Your busy season doesn't block us."
- **"Will we need new technology?"** → "Minimal system changes. This is process redesign primarily. If you decide to modernize GL later, that's separate."
- **"What's your success rate?"** → "12+ close transformations; 8+ in similar-size companies. Average time to 6-day close is 4 months; cost is $400-600K. All engagements delivered on time and within budget."

### Visuals guidance
- Use simple, clean slides (white background, limited colors, sans-serif fonts)
- Icons > walls of text
- Data visualizations (bar charts, process flows, waterfall) > tables
- Avoid dense tables; use callout numbers instead
- Customize company logo placement and colors

## Slide deck checklist

Before presenting:
- [ ] Title slide has client name, engagement title, date, confidentiality
- [ ] Agenda slide (Slide 2) previews structure
- [ ] Problem clearly quantified (days, effort %, findings)
- [ ] Hypothesis is crisp (one or two sentences, testable)
- [ ] Approach timeline is clear (4 phases, 17 weeks total)
- [ ] Value is quantified (dollar amount, ROI)
- [ ] Risks are finance-specific, not generic
- [ ] Case pattern is comparable (similar size, industry, complexity)
- [ ] Team is credible (relevant experience, similar engagements)
- [ ] Investment and ROI clear (cost, payback period)
- [ ] Next steps explicit (decision timeline, kickoff plan)
- [ ] Deck doesn't exceed 18 slides (focus is enforced by slide limit)
- [ ] No credential inflation before problem is established
- [ ] Tone is confident, problem-led, not self-promotional
