# Quality Checklist — Pre-Finalization Verification

Run this checklist before finalizing any proposal or pitch deck. All items must be green (✓) before presentation.

---

## Content Quality

- [ ] **Problem statement comes first** — Before any firm credentials or "why us", open with the client's specific finance challenge
  - Fix if: First page talks about Deloitte or your team
  - Example: "Your month-end close has grown from 8 to 14 days..." NOT "Deloitte has transformed 500+ finance functions..."

- [ ] **Problem is quantified with specific metrics** — Not "you have delays" but "12-day close vs. 4-day benchmark"
  - Fix if: Problem described in general terms (e.g., "challenges with month-end close")
  - Verify: Every problem statement includes numbers (days, %, headcount, cost, findings)

- [ ] **Root cause analysis is specific, not generic** — Identifies actual causes (data quality, organization, process), not "legacy systems" hand-waving
  - Fix if: Root cause is "inefficient processes" or "lack of automation"
  - Verify: Root cause broken into 2-3 specific categories (process bottlenecks, data quality, organizational structure, technology constraints)

- [ ] **Insight is rooted in root cause analysis** — Not "we've seen this before" but specific to their organization/data/process
  - Fix if: "We have experience with similar challenges"
  - Verify: Insight references specific findings from YOUR current-state assessment (e.g., "your 15% unmapped GL accounts...")

- [ ] **Hypothesis is testable and crisp** — One or two sentences, not a paragraph
  - Fix if: "We will implement a modern finance operating model using cloud technology and best-in-class automation to improve close speed and efficiency"
  - Verify: Hypothesis states (1) target outcome, (2) timeframe, (3) how you'll get there, (4) estimated value

- [ ] **Every metric in proposal is CFO-relevant** — Close days, forecast %, DSO/DPO, cost %, control issues — not vanity metrics
  - Fix if: Proposal mentions "% of automation achieved" or "system migration completion"
  - Verify: Every metric answers "Why does the CFO care about this?"

- [ ] **Value case has 3+ levers** — Not just cost reduction; includes working capital, revenue, risk, or capability value
  - Fix if: "We will save $500K in FTE costs"
  - Verify: At least 3 value drivers; total value clearly quantified in dollars

---

## Story Arc & Narrative

- [ ] **Five-step arc is clear** — Situation → Insight → Hypothesis → What we'll do → Why us
  - Verify: Can reader trace each step?
  - Fix if: Steps are jumbled or missing

- [ ] **Client situation comes before firm credentials** — Client problem establishes credibility, not your firm history
  - Verify: CFO problem appears in first 3 pages; your firm's experience appears later (pages 5+)
  - Fix if: Pages 1-3 are about Deloitte/your firm

- [ ] **"Why us" section is proportional** — 5-10% of document, not 30%
  - Fix if: "Why us" takes 10+ pages, or dominates the narrative
  - Verify: One strong case pattern + brief team credential statement

- [ ] **Tone is confident, not arrogant** — "We know how to solve this" not "We are the only firm that can"
  - Fix if: Language like "world-class," "best-in-class," "global leader" dominates
  - Verify: Confidence comes from specific experience, not credential inflation

---

## Anti-patterns Avoided

- [ ] **NO generic "digital transformation" language** — Finance-specific language throughout
  - Fix if: Phrases like "modernize processes," "leverage automation," "enhance visibility"
  - Verify: Use finance-specific terms (GL reconciliation, close calendar, DSO, DPO, control findings)

- [ ] **NO credential inflation before problem established** — Firm credentials come AFTER problem is clear
  - Fix if: Opens with "Deloitte has...", "Our global network...", award mentions
  - Verify: First 2-3 pages focus entirely on CLIENT problem, not your firm

- [ ] **NO tech-first solutions** — Process/organization are the levers; tech is enabler
  - Fix if: Hypothesis is "implement new GL system" or "migrate to cloud"
  - Verify: Hypothesis is process redesign or organization change; tech is secondary

- [ ] **NO value case with only cost takeout** — Include working capital, risk, or revenue levers
  - Fix if: "We will save $400K in FTE costs" with nothing else
  - Verify: At least 3 value drivers quantified

- [ ] **NO generic risks** — Risks are finance-specific with concrete mitigations
  - Fix if: "Communication risk," "resource availability risk," "schedule risk"
  - Verify: Risks are specific to finance (data quality, staff resistance, audit timing) with tailored mitigations

- [ ] **NO vague mitigations** — Mitigations are concrete, not "daily communication" or "strong governance"
  - Fix if: "We will establish governance" or "maintain close communication"
  - Verify: Mitigation includes specific action (e.g., "2-week data remediation sprint," "change management program with reskilling")

- [ ] **NO unrealistic timelines** — Phases include contingency and decision gates
  - Fix if: "We will achieve 6-day close in 2 months"
  - Verify: Timeline includes 10-15% contingency; decision gates after each phase

---

## Approach & Delivery

- [ ] **Phased approach is clear** — Distinct phases with deliverables, decision gates, client commitments
  - Verify: Reader understands what happens in each phase, what client must provide, when go/no-go decision is made
  - Fix if: Phases are unclear or sequential without decision gates

- [ ] **Client commitments are specific** — Not "team support" but "Close owner 50% time, GL manager 25%"
  - Verify: Each phase lists FTE commitment by role
  - Fix if: "Client will provide necessary resources"

- [ ] **Decision gates are clear** — Each phase ends with explicit go/no-go decision
  - Verify: Phase ends with "Decision gate: Approve [deliverable]? Proceed to next phase?"
  - Fix if: Phases flow into each other with no pause points

- [ ] **Team is credible** — Specific relevant experience, not generic bios
  - Fix if: "Jane is a skilled finance transformation consultant with 10 years experience"
  - Verify: "Jane led 8 close accelerations at mid-market manufacturers in the past 3 years; she was transformation lead on [case pattern company]"

---

## Value Case Quality

- [ ] **Value is quantified in dollars** — Not percentages, not "significant improvement"
  - Fix if: "$2M+ annual value" not "substantial cost savings" or "25% FTE reduction"
  - Verify: Value stated as "$X million" or "$X thousand" in total and per-lever

- [ ] **Value realization timeline is clear** — When each benefit is realized (Month 1, 3, 6, 12)
  - Verify: Reader understands which benefits are immediate vs. phased
  - Fix if: Value is stated as "annual run-rate" without clarifying when it starts

- [ ] **ROI is calculated** — Investment cost vs. Year 1 value, or multi-year payback period
  - Fix if: No ROI calculation present
  - Verify: "Investment: $500K | Year 1 Value: $2M+ | ROI: 4x" or "Payback period: 2 months"

- [ ] **Value is credible** — Conservative assumptions; not over-optimized
  - Fix if: Assumes 100% automation of GL reconciliation, or 0 effort post-go-live
  - Verify: Assumptions are realistic and conservative (e.g., 70% of possible benefit, not 100%)

- [ ] **Benchmarks are labeled as illustrative** — If client numbers not provided, clearly marked [illustrative]
  - Fix if: "Best-in-class close is 4 days" without context
  - Verify: "[Illustrative] Best-in-class close for mid-market manufacturers is 4-5 days"

---

## Risk Management

- [ ] **Risk register includes 4+ risks** — Not generic; specific to finance transformation
  - Verify: Risks are data quality, staff resistance, audit timing, tech constraints, scope creep, etc.
  - Fix if: "Communication risk," "resource risk," "schedule risk" only

- [ ] **Risks are finance-specific** — Not generic project risks
  - Fix if: Risks apply to ANY project (supply chain, IT, HR, etc.)
  - Verify: Risks are unique to finance (GL data quality, approval workflow change, audit season, system architecture)

- [ ] **Each risk has clear impact & probability** — "High impact if GL data quality blocks automation"
  - Verify: Impact is quantified (revenue impact, timeline impact, cost impact)
  - Fix if: "Significant impact if..." without numbers

- [ ] **Each risk has concrete mitigation** — Not "we'll monitor closely" but "2-week data remediation sprint"
  - Verify: Mitigation is specific action, not vague governance
  - Fix if: "We will establish strong governance" or "maintain close oversight"

- [ ] **Risk ownership is assigned** — "Owner: Finance + IT" or "Owner: CFO + Project Manager"
  - Verify: Reader knows who is accountable for mitigating each risk
  - Fix if: "We will mitigate..." without clear owner

---

## Proposal Structure & Formatting

- [ ] **Executive summary is self-contained** — Can be read independently; answers who/what/when/how much
  - Verify: Reader understands problem, approach, value, timeline, next steps from exec summary alone
  - Fix if: Exec summary references sections that haven't been read yet

- [ ] **10-section framework is mapped** — All sections present or explicitly scoped as "optional"
  - Verify: Reader can find each section (client understanding, approach, solution, value, risks, etc.)
  - Fix if: Sections are missing or merged without clear delineation

- [ ] **Visuals support narrative** — Diagrams, charts, process flows aid understanding
  - Verify: Each key section has a visual (not just text)
  - Fix if: Pages 5-10 are solid text with no diagrams

- [ ] **Length is appropriate** — Pitch deck 12-18 slides, full proposal 50-80 pages
  - Verify: Deck not more than 18 slides; proposal not more than 80 pages
  - Fix if: Deck has 25+ slides (too long for executive meeting); proposal has 150 pages (too detailed)

- [ ] **Confidentiality language is present** — "Confidential" marked on cover and all pages
  - Verify: Marked on cover, header/footer, and appendices
  - Fix if: Missing from any section

---

## Case Patterns & Proof

- [ ] **At least one case pattern included** — Comparable company, baseline metrics, results, timeline
  - Verify: Case pattern has all components (company profile, baseline, approach, results)
  - Fix if: No case pattern, or case pattern is vague

- [ ] **Case pattern is comparable** — Similar size, industry, complexity, geography
  - Verify: Client can see themselves in the case pattern
  - Fix if: Case pattern is much larger/smaller, or different industry

- [ ] **Case pattern is specific** — Numbers, timeline, key learning (not generic success story)
  - Fix if: "We successfully transformed their close process"
  - Verify: "For a $2B specialty chemicals company, we reduced close from 12 to 6 days in 4 months. Key learning: data remediation was the real bottleneck, not technology."

- [ ] **Case pattern is anonymized** — No company name, but enough context to be credible
  - Verify: Reader knows size, industry, geography; doesn't know company name
  - Fix if: Company name disclosed, or anonymization is so vague case pattern is unbelievable

---

## Proposal Tone & Language

- [ ] **Third-person, confident tone** — Not "I can help" or "you should"
  - Fix if: "We can help you achieve...", "You might consider..."
  - Verify: "We will reduce close from 12 to 6 days through..." (confident, clear)

- [ ] **Finance-specific terminology** — Close calendar, GL reconciliation, DSO, approval workflow, control findings
  - Fix if: "Optimize processes," "improve efficiency," "enhance visibility"
  - Verify: Every section uses precise finance language

- [ ] **No superlatives without evidence** — Not "best-in-class," "world-class," "innovative"
  - Fix if: "We bring world-class expertise" or "Our innovative solution"
  - Verify: Claims supported by evidence (case patterns, specific experience)

- [ ] **Consistent terminology throughout** — Pick one term and use it consistently
  - Verify: Don't mix "outcomes" and "benefits," or "value case" and "ROI"
  - Fix if: Same concept called different names in different sections

---

## Final Read-Through (Do this last)

Run through the entire proposal as if you're the CFO:

- [ ] **Would I believe this is about MY problem?** — Does problem statement match my situation?
- [ ] **Would I believe they understand my constraints?** — Risk register and approach show deep understanding?
- [ ] **Would I believe they can deliver this?** — Case patterns and team prove capability?
- [ ] **Would I believe the value?** — Value case is credible and conservative?
- [ ] **Would I want to move forward?** — Clear next steps and timeline?

If you answer YES to all 5, proposal is ready. If NO to any, fix before sending.

## Sign-Off Checklist

Before final submission to client:

- [ ] Grammar & spell-check complete
- [ ] All internal comments removed
- [ ] All template language replaced with client-specific content
- [ ] Page numbers and table of contents accurate
- [ ] All hyperlinks functional (if PDF)
- [ ] Confidentiality marking on all pages
- [ ] Appendices complete and properly referenced
- [ ] Margins consistent
- [ ] Visuals are clear and professional
- [ ] No placeholder text (e.g., "[INSERT VALUE HERE]")
- [ ] Review by peer consultant (for quality check)
- [ ] Review by engagement manager or partner (for accuracy & tone)
- [ ] Date, version number, and author clearly marked

## If you fail any check...

**Weak problem statement?**
→ Go back to current-state assessment; what's the real pain? Quantify it.

**Weak hypothesis?**
→ Tighten to one sentence. What's the single biggest lever? What's realistic?

**Value case weak?**
→ Add a second or third lever. Every finance transformation has cost + working capital + risk value.

**Risks feel generic?**
→ Identify constraints specific to THIS client and THIS engagement. What could actually go wrong?

**Why us weak?**
→ Find one concrete case pattern. Quantified outcome. What surprised you about that engagement?

**Tone off?**
→ Remove all adjectives that aren't supported (best-in-class, innovative, world-class). Rewrite with specific facts and evidence.

**Timeline unrealistic?**
→ Add contingency. Add decision gates. Be honest about ramp-up periods.
