## Document Structure

### Front Matter (3-5 pages)

**Cover Page**
- Client name and logo
- Engagement title (e.g., "Finance Close Acceleration Transformation")
- Date
- Confidentiality statement

**Table of Contents**
- Auto-generated from headings
- Bookmark to enable navigation in PDF

**Executive Summary (2-3 pages)**
- Problem statement (1 paragraph): "Your month-end close has grown from 8 to 14 days, limiting strategic decision-making"
- Headline opportunity (1 paragraph): "We believe you can achieve 6-day close in 4 months through process redesign and targeted automation, delivering $2M+ annual value"
- Approach overview (1 paragraph): "4-phase approach starting with diagnose & design, followed by build, pilot, and deployment"
- Value summary (table): Cost, working capital, risk reduction by initiative
- Risk summary (table): Key risks and mitigations
- Next steps: "This proposal is good for 30 days; we'll schedule a follow-up to discuss next steps"

### Section 1: Client Understanding (5-8 pages)

**Problem statement**
- Current close calendar (12 days) vs. benchmark (4-5 days)
- Impact: Late P&L delays CFO strategic decisions, reduces agility
- Evidence: Quote from CFO or Controller describing impact

**Current-state assessment**
- Close process overview (diagram or table of major tasks)
- GL reconciliation (45% of close effort, currently manual)
- Approval workflow (8 sign-offs, some redundant)
- Systems landscape (SAP, consolidation tool, manual reconciliation tools)
- Team (4 FTE dedicated, pulled to other priorities)

**Root cause analysis** (table or narrative)
- GL data quality: 15% of accounts unmapped; subledger-to-GL mismatch drives manual rework
- Process design: 8 approval steps, sequential (not parallel)
- Organization: Close owner is 10% role; insufficient dedicated resources
- Technology: GL reconciliation tool is Excel; limited automation capability

**Benchmarking** (table or chart)
- Current close calendar (12 days) vs. industry best-in-class (4-5 days)
- GL reconciliation effort (45% of close) vs. best-in-class (<5% of close)
- Finance cost (0.9% of revenue) vs. best-in-class (0.6% of revenue)

**Constraints & dependencies**
- Year-end audit cycle (Oct-Dec) limits implementation window
- SAP system refresh schedule (GL batch job runs nightly, limits real-time visibility)
- Organizational readiness (staff skeptical of process change)

### Section 2: Our Hypothesis & Approach (5-8 pages)

**Our point of view**
- Paragraph 1: Root causes are 70% process/organization, 30% technology (not tech-first)
- Paragraph 2: Opportunity to achieve 6-day close through process redesign + targeted GL reconciliation automation, not full GL modernization
- Paragraph 3: Timeline 4 months; requires client commitment to phase project around audit calendar

**Phased approach** (with narrative + timeline diagram)

**Phase 1: Diagnose & Design (Weeks 1-4)**
- Current-state detailed assessment
- Root cause analysis
- Design GL reconciliation automation rules
- Streamline approval workflow
- **Deliverables:** Current-state documentation, design document, project plan
- **Client commitment:** Close owner 50%, GL manager 25%
- **Decision gate:** Approve design; assess data quality; decide quick-win vs. full transformation

**Phase 2: Build & Configure (Weeks 5-10)**
- Data remediation (unmapped accounts, GL hierarchy)
- Update close procedures
- Configure GL reconciliation automation
- Prepare training materials
- **Deliverables:** Remediated data, updated procedures, training materials
- **Client commitment:** Close owner 75%, GL manager 50%, IT support
- **Decision gate:** Data quality passes validation; procedures approved; proceed to pilot

**Phase 3: Pilot & Stabilize (Weeks 11-14)**
- Run full close cycle with new process
- Test GL reconciliation automation
- Refine procedures based on pilot results
- Train full team
- **Deliverables:** Pilot results, refined procedures, training completion
- **Client commitment:** Close owner 100%, full team in pilot close
- **Decision gate:** Pilot achieves target (6 days)? Proceed to full deployment

**Phase 4: Deploy & Transition (Weeks 15-17)**
- Execute full close with new process
- 24/7 support during first close
- Measure actual performance
- Transition to business-as-usual
- **Deliverables:** Go-live results, final procedures, staffing model
- **Client commitment:** Close owner 100%, escalation available
- **Timeline:** 17 weeks total; 4 months to full capability

### Section 3: What We Will Change (5-10 pages, customize per engagement type)

**Process changes**
- Current workflow (diagram): 8 approvals, 12 days
- Future workflow (diagram): 2 approvals, 6 days
- Specific changes:
  - Remove 2 redundant approval steps (consolidation approval, corporate approval)
  - Enable parallel regional closings (3-day compression)
  - Automated GL reconciliation (eliminate manual 5-day task)

**Organization & Staffing**
- Current: Close owner is 10% role, 3 part-time GL staff
- Future: Dedicated 1 FTE close owner, 1 FTE GL specialist, 2 FTE transactional close
- Roles & responsibilities (table): New role definitions, what's different

**Technology & Tools**
- Current: Manual GL reconciliation (Excel); no real-time visibility
- Future: GL reconciliation automation (rules-based, 90% auto-posting); real-time close tracking dashboard
- No full GL modernization required; process discipline + targeted automation
- System changes minimal (GL refresh schedule, reconciliation rules setup, reporting)

**Data & Controls**
- Current: 15% of GL accounts unmapped; subledger-to-GL reconciliation manual
- Future: 100% account mapping, automated reconciliation, control automation for high-risk accounts
- Manual controls remain for exception handling (30% of accounts need judgment)

### Section 6: Risks & Mitigations (3-5 pages)

**Risk register** (table format)

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Data quality blocks automation** | GL recon benefit drops from 35% to 15% | Medium (60%) | HIGH | 2-week data cleanse pre-transformation |
| **Staff resistance to approval removal** | Late adoption; process degradation | High (80%) | HIGH | Change mgmt program + reskilling |
| **Audit calendar conflicts** | Implementation delayed 1-2 months | Very High (100%) | HIGH | Phase project around audit |
| **GL system constraints** | Real-time recon not possible | Medium (30%) | MEDIUM | Early tech assessment + alternative design |
| **Scope creep** | Timeline slips; budget overruns | High (70%) | MEDIUM | Strict change control; decide "in/out" at design gate |

**Risk mitigation strategies**
- Data quality: Pre-transformation audit (Week 1); remediation (Weeks 5-7)
- Staff resistance: Change management program starting Week 2; incentive alignment; reskilling offered
- Audit calendar: Project phases around Oct-Dec; deploy Jan-Feb post-audit
- Technical constraints: System architecture review Week 1; alternative approaches identified Week 2
- Scope control: Change control process; sponsor approval required for changes

**Contingency & escalation**
- If critical risk realized (e.g., severe data quality issues), escalate to sponsor for decision on scope/timeline adjustment
- 10% schedule contingency (17 weeks baseline; up to 19 weeks if contingency used)
- 15% budget contingency for unexpected issues

### Section 8: Case Patterns (3-5 pages)

**Case 1: Close Acceleration — Manufacturing**
- Company: $2B+ specialty chemicals company
- Baseline: 12-day close, 45% GL recon effort, 6 SAP control findings
- Approach: Process redesign + GL reconciliation automation
- Results: 6-day close (4-month timeline), 35% FTE savings, 0 SAP findings by Month 6
- Lessons: Data remediation critical; staff resistance overcome through change mgmt

**Case 2: Close Acceleration — Technology**
- Company: $1.5B SaaS company
- Baseline: 10-day close, 40% GL recon effort, 4 control findings
- Approach: Process streamlining + NetSuite GL automation
- Results: 5-day close (3-month timeline), 25% FTE savings, full SOX remediation
- Lessons: Cloud systems enable faster automation; technology fit better than legacy SAP

**Case 3: GBS Transformation — Financial Services**
- Company: $5B financial services firm
- Baseline: Finance cost 0.9% of revenue, 30% GBS penetration
- Approach: Transition AP/GL to GBS, automation of transactional processes
- Results: 0.6% of revenue (33% cost reduction), 70% GBS penetration
- Lessons: Phased transition critical; staff ramp-up longer than expected (6 months vs. 3)
